💡 How It Works (Simple Explanation)

Think of Amazon SES as a super-efficient postal service for email. Your website gives the letter (email) to this service, which then delivers it at lightning speed to millions of people worldwide. This extension is the smart receptionist that:

  1. Takes your emails - Collects messages from your website or applications
  2. Prepares them perfectly - Handles all the technical formatting (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) so email providers trust you
  3. Sends them through Amazon's network - Uses Amazon's powerful, reliable infrastructure with 99.9% uptime
  4. Reports back everything - Tells you exactly what happened with every single email
